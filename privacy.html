Privacy Policy

FullPage PDF Shot — Chrome extension

Last updated: 2026-09-30

Summary

FullPage PDF Shot captures webpages on your device so you can preview and export them. Screenshot processing happens locally in the browser extension. We do not operate a custom backend that receives your screenshots.

What we process locally

  • Visible-tab screenshots used to build a full-page image
  • Temporary capture previews stored in IndexedDB on your device
  • A random device identifier and license status in chrome.storage.local

License activation

If you activate a license, your license key (and related public license-validation fields) are sent to Dodo Payments over HTTPS so the extension can activate and validate the key. That traffic goes to Dodo Payments (test.dodopayments.com or live.dodopayments.com), not to a custom server operated by this extension.

What we do not do

  • We do not upload page screenshots to our own servers (there is no custom screenshot backend)
  • We do not sell personal data
  • We do not use analytics SDKs in this extension build

Permissions

The extension uses activeTab/scripting/tabs to scroll and capture the current page, storage for license state and local captures, and host access to call Dodo Payments license APIs and to capture pages you choose to photograph.

Contact

For privacy questions about this extension, contact the publisher listed on the Chrome Web Store listing for FullPage PDF Shot.
